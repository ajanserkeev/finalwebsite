| Q-001 | Should the MVP payment flow collect **only the 15–20% platform deposit** online (Bolshaya Strana style, where the tourist pays the remaining 80–85% to the organizer on arrival in Kyrgyzstan), or **100% full tour prepayment** (Viator style, with automated payout to the operator)? | Collecting only the 15–20% deposit online eliminates escrow/tax liability on the remaining 80–85% of the tour volume, reduces payment gateway fees by 5x, and matches how Kyrgyz tour operators prefer to receive cash/transfers on arrival. | Founder decision before Phase 2 payment integration. | open |
| Q-002 | Should tourists be able to message the Tour Organizer **before** paying the deposit (via an on-platform Q&A chat that masks phone numbers/links), or can they submit a **free booking application ("Оставить заявку")** first and pay the deposit after operator confirmation? | Bolshaya Strana uses "Submit Application -> Operator/Manager confirms seat -> Pay Deposit Link", which has much higher conversion for $500–$1,500 multi-day mountain tours than cold checkout. | Founder decision for MVP checkout flow (`FL-001`). | open |
| Q-003 | Which legal entity & acquiring provider in the Kyrgyz Republic (e.g., **Finik** vs. **Freedom Pay** vs. **Optima/Eldik Bank** internet acquiring) will be used at launch to accept foreign Visa/Mastercard alongside local MBANK/Elkart? | Directly determines API integration time and cross-border card acceptance rate for US/EU/GCC/CIS tourists. | Consult Freedom Pay KG & Finik onboarding teams with a registered KR IE (ИП) or LLC (ОсОО). | open |
| Q-004 | What legal clauses are required in the Public Offer (Публичная оферта) and Operator Agency Contract under Kyrgyz Republic law so the platform acts strictly as an **information/agency bridge** while liability for on-the-ground tour execution remains with the Organizer? | Protects the platform from liability for mountain weather delays, transport changes, or organizer force majeure in Kyrgyzstan. | Review with a Kyrgyz commercial/tourism lawyer before onboarding external operators. | open |
