# Glossary

| Term | Definition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Direct Price Guarantee (Гарантия прямых цен) | Core rule from Bolshaya Strana where the tour price on the aggregator is strictly identical to (or lower than) the organizer's own website/Instagram price; the platform earns an agency commission (10–20%) out of the organizer's retail price rather than adding a markup on top. | [doc SRC-001] |
| Open Organizer Profile (Открытый профиль организатора) | Unlike opaque OTAs that hide the local operator until after payment, every tour card and detail page openly displays the organizer's brand name, photo/logo, legal verification badge, years of experience, aggregate rating, and completed tour count. | [doc SRC-001] |
| Departure Group / Slot (Дата заезда / Набор группы) | A specific scheduled run of a multi-day tour (`start_date` to `end_date`) with a defined `max_Seats` capacity, `seats_Remaining` counter, per-departure price, and status (`available`, `few_seats`, `guaranteed`, `sold_out`, `closed`). | [doc SRC-001] |
| Guaranteed Departure (Гарантированный заезд) | Badge on a departure date indicating the minimum group size (`min_Participants`) has already been met or the organizer commits to running the tour regardless of additional signups, removing cancellation anxiety for tourists buying flights. | [doc SRC-001] |
| Deposit / Prepayment Split (Предоплата / Аванс) | Booking payment model (standard on Bolshaya Strana) where the tourist pays a 15%–25% deposit online on the platform to lock in seats (which covers or secures the platform's commission), and pays the remaining 75%–85% directly to the organizer (e.g., on arrival in Bishkek/Karakol or X days before departure). | [doc SRC-002] |
| Difficulty Level (Уровень сложности) | Standardized 4- or 5-tier physical exertion rating (1: Easy/Sightseeing, 2: Moderate/Light walks, 3: Active/Day hikes & horseback, 4: Challenging/High-altitude trekking 3000m+, 5: Expedition/Mountaineering) crucial for Kyrgyzstan mountain tours. | [doc SRC-001] |
| Comfort Level (Уровень комфорта) | Standardized accommodation & amenities scale (Basic/Tents, Nomadic/Shared Yurt Camp, Standard/Guesthouses with facilities, Comfort/Boutique Hotels & Glamping). | [doc SRC-001] |
| Booking Cutoff (Срок закрытия бронирования) | Viator supplier rule defining the minimum hours/days before departure (`cutoff_Hours`) when online self-service booking automatically locks out. | [doc SRC-006] |
| Verified Tourist Review (Проверенный отзыв туриста) | A review submitted via a unique post-trip token issued only to tourists with a `completed` booking for that specific organizer and tour, optionally containing traveler photos and an official Organizer Reply. | [doc SRC-006] |
| Partner / Operator Portal (Личный кабинет организатора) | Dedicated web dashboard where an approved tour operator manages their tour programs, departure dates/seat quotas, sees real-time counters of **tours sold**, **people signed up (participant manifests)**, revenue/commission breakdown, and responds to tourist inquiries/reviews. | [doc SRC-003] |
