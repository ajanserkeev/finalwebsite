# Implications for the clone

## Copy

| Area | Item | Why | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pricing & Trust (`Bolshaya Strana`) | **100% Direct Organizer Price Guarantee (0% Markup)** with visible badge & price-match link | Eliminates the #1 reason tourists abandon aggregators (fear of middleman markup); aligns perfectly with your vision of matching the organizer's direct price | F-004, R-002, SRC-001 |
| Transparency (`Bolshaya Strana`) | **Open Organizer Identity Card (`C-003`) & Dedicated Organizer Public Page (`S-004`)** | Showing the real local operator's brand, verification status, completed tours, and aggregated reviews builds immediate trust for both the tourist and the local partner | F-003, R-001, S-004 |
| Checkout & Economics (`Bolshaya Strana`) | **15%–20% Online Deposit Split (`R-003`)** (Deposit paid online to platform; remaining 80%–85% paid directly to organizer on arrival) | Reduces tourist checkout friction on $600–$1,500 multi-day tours, slashes payment gateway fees by 5×, and automatically collects the platform's agency commission upfront without complex escrow payouts! | F-005, R-003, SRC-002 |
| Inventory (`Bolshaya Strana`) | **Group Departure Schedule & Seat Quota Matrix (`C-004`)** with `"Guaranteed Departure"` badge and exact `"X of 12 spots left"` | Multi-day tours in Kyrgyzstan (Song-Kul, Kel-Suu, Karakol) run on fixed group departure dates; tourists need to see which dates are guaranteed before booking flights to Bishkek | F-006, R-004, S-003 |
| Social Proof (`Viator`) | **Automated Post-Trip Verified Review Engine (`FL-003`)** with trip photos & official Organizer Reply | Only allowing reviews from completed bookings (`R-007`) makes reviews 100% trustworthy | F-007, R-007, R-008, SRC-006 |
| Partner Portal (`Viator` + `Bolshaya Strana`) | **Dedicated Tour Operator Portal (`S-006`)** with **Tours Sold**, **People Signed Up (`Pax`)**, Departure Fill Bars & CSV/PDF Participant Manifests | Gives every partner complete visibility into their sales, participant lists, and remaining seats | F-009, F-010, F-011, R-009 |

## Avoid

| Area | Item | Why | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organizer Visibility (`Viator`) | **Hiding or burying the Tour Operator's name until after payment (`B-001`)** | Directly contradicts your transparent bridge-aggregator positioning; tourists want to know which local Kyrgyz team is taking them into the mountains | B-001, R-001 |
| Supplier Fees (`Viator`) | **Charging a $29 upfront listing fee per tour or 25%+ commission (`B-002`)** | Causes local Kyrgyz operators to inflate their prices on the platform above their Instagram/website price, destroying price parity | B-002, R-002 |
| Checkout Flow (`Viator`) | **Forcing 100% upfront online prepayment on expensive multi-day expeditions (`B-004`)** | Creates massive refund/escrow friction and 3%+ card processing loss on the entire tour price | B-004, R-003 |

## Improve

| Area | Item | How | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seat Quota Accuracy (`B-003`) | **1-Click Mobile Quota Sync + Telegram Bot for Kyrgyz Operators** | Let operators adjust `seatsRemaining` (`[-]` / `[+]`) in 1 tap from their phone (`S-006`) or via a connected Telegram bot whenever they sell a spot on WhatsApp/Instagram | B-003, F-010, S-006 |
| Rural Comfort Transparency (`B-005`) | **Kyrgyzstan-Specific Accommodation & Road Reality Matrix on `S-003`** | Explicitly show per night whether accommodation is a Felt Yurt Camp (shared outdoor/heated washroom, altitude 3016m), Guesthouse, or 3–4* Hotel, plus driving hours vs. off-road 4WD hours | B-005, F-002, S-003 |
| Local + Global Payments (`BR-001`) | **Dual Payment Rail (International Visa/MC + Local MBANK / Finik QR)** | Serve both international tourists (USD/EUR cards) and CIS/domestic travelers (MBANK, Elkart, RUB/KGS) seamlessly | BR-001, F-005, S-005 |
